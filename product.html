♡ 10 Custom / 24 Universal Nails

  • Step 1: Gently push back cuticles with the wooden stick and lightly buff the natural nail surface.
  • Step 2: Cleanse nails thoroughly with the included alcohol wipe to remove natural oils.
  • Step 3: Apply a drop of glue (for 2-3 weeks wear) OR sticky tabs (for weekend 3-4 days wear).
  • Step 4: Align nail at 45° from cuticle line and press firmly down for 25-30 seconds. Done!
  • Removal: Soak hands in warm soapy water with a drop of cuticle oil for 10-15 minutes. Gently peel off from sides.
